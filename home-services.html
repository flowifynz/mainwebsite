QUESTIONS SOLO OPERATORS ACTUALLY ASK

Straight answers, before you book.

Do I need Jobber or any other job management software?+

No. Flowify Signal runs on a simple lead pipeline built for solo and small-crew operators — no software to learn, we run it for you.

Can it handle recurring rounds, like weekly mowing?+

Yes — recurring jobs and one-off jobs both flow through the same pipeline, and the calendar handles the recurring side from there.

What if I want to add invoicing or GST tracking?+

You can keep your own Xero separately if you need GST-compliant records — Flowify Signal doesn't require it, and the pipeline's own invoicing may be enough for the most bare-bones setup.

I already answer some calls myself. Does the receptionist replace me?+

No — it catches what you'd otherwise miss when you're out on a job. Calls you do answer yourself still go through you as normal.

What if I grow and need full dispatch later?+

We move you up to Flowify Core when the job calls for it — same receptionist, same number, same customer history, just a bigger system underneath.
