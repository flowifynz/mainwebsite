QUESTIONS TRADIES ACTUALLY ASK

Straight answers, before you book.

Do I have to learn new software?+

No. We build it, connect it, and run it. If you ever want to look at your job board or your numbers, you can — but nothing about your day-to-day requires you to touch the backend.

What happens if the AI receptionist gets something wrong?+

Every call is monitored, and you can review any conversation. If something's off — a wrong detail, a missed nuance — we adjust the script within days, not weeks.

Is this locked into a long contract?+

Setup is a one-off fee; the monthly plan runs month-to-month. We'd rather earn the retainer every month than trap you in a contract you want out of.

Do I need Jobber or Xero already?+

No — if you're on Flowify Core or Pro & Scale, we set those up as part of the build. If you're on Flowify Signal, you don't need them at all.

What if I outgrow Signal and need full dispatch later?+

We move you up to Core when the job calls for it — same receptionist, same number, same customer history, just a bigger system underneath.
