What tradies actually ask us

How much is an AI receptionist worth to a trade business?+

Most tradies we talk to lose two to five jobs a week to missed calls alone. At an average job value, that's usually the setup cost recovered inside the first month.

Is an AI receptionist actually worth it, or just another subscription?+

It's worth it if missed calls are costing you jobs — which, for most trade businesses we've worked with, they are. It's not worth it if you already answer every call yourself and never miss one.

How does the AI receptionist actually work?+

It answers your calls in a natural NZ accent, asks the same questions a good office manager would, then logs the job straight into your system and texts the caller a confirmation. No app for your customers, no change to your phone number.

Can AI really replace a receptionist for a trade business?+

Not entirely — it doesn't build relationships with your regulars the way a person can. What it does is make sure nobody hits voicemail, at any hour, which is where most tradies actually lose the job.
